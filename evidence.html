Evidence register

What is established, and what is not.

Claims are classified individually, not by page and not by product. Nothing inherits a status from the programme as a whole. Null, invalid and unsupported results appear here with the same visibility as supporting ones.

Definitions

Two separate axes.

Evidence status answers what has been shown. Maturity answers what exists and how you can get it. A thing can be fully implemented and still have no evidence of benefit.

Established within stated scope

Qualifying completed evidence and an explicitly identified independent reproduction or confirmation route. Population, instrument and claim boundary are stated.

Observed under a frozen study

A completed preregistered or otherwise frozen experiment, without qualifying independent reproduction.

Under active investigation

A frozen experiment or implementation test exists, but has no qualifying result yet.

Not established

No qualifying evidence exists, or the claim was null, invalid, unsupported or outside the measured scope.

Preregistration strengthens evidential integrity but does not by itself establish a result. “Independent” is not a synonym for “external”, and neither is silently substituted for the other.

Public release

Externally obtainable; exact version, installation route, supported workflows and limitations verifiable.

Research implementation

Implemented and exercised inside the governed programme; not the currently available public product.

Target architecture

Intended structure or capability, not yet established as a complete public or research implementation.
