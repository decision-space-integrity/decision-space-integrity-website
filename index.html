Where this started.

PREPRINT · OPEN MATERIALS

Decision-Space Collapse in Advisory Language Models

A frozen 6,480-output empirical study of trajectory omission, framing sensitivity and recovery in advisory model outputs, with its preprint, DOI and public replication materials.

This work measures visibility of configured expected paths in model outputs. It does not measure advice quality, factual correctness, user outcomes, or regulatory compliance.

Compression is not automatically pathological collapse. Shortening an answer is normal; a transformation only creates the opportunity for loss. What DSI measures is whether structure a governed reference says should remain visible actually did.

Measurement integrity

If the ruler changes, what does the number mean?

If the reference, population or evaluator changes, did the system improve — or did the ruler change?

Building the instrument surfaced a second problem underneath the first: a change to admission, reference construction, sensors or authority can alter what a metric means without altering the number. That is why measurement identity and comparability are governed separately.
