Pluraxis (target architecture).

A target system designed to explore options before narrowing, with separate measurement and authority.

Target architectureEfficacy: not established

Individual components exist at different maturity levels. The integrated system, and any effect it may have on decision quality, are not established.

Three different things

Kept apart deliberately.

UI & INTEGRATION COMPONENTS   Research implementation

Interaction and integration components are locally implemented and covered by tests. That evidence is locally reported; it has not been independently reproduced.

INTEGRATED PLURAXIS · DSI · GATE   Target architecture

The joined operation — explore, measure, then dispose under authority — is intended structure, not an established system.

DELIBERATION-LOOP EFFICACY   Not established

Whether the loop improves anything for a person deciding is a claim, not a component. No qualifying result exists, and none is asserted here.
